TCSLib Project

Jan 2026 – Present

Research Assistant · Formal verification in Lean

With Professor Venkatesan Guruswami and Shilun Li, I help build a library of formalized theoretical computer science in Lean. My work focuses on the analysis of Boolean functions: formalizing essential definitions and lemmas, verifying results on hypercontractivity and threshold functions, and helping implement AI-based methods that accelerate the formalization process.

HEAT Lab

Nov 2024 – Present

Research Assistant · Turbulent flow modeling and data analysis

At Berkeley's HEAT Lab, led by Professor Guanyu Su, I work on fiber-optic temperature sensing for sodium-cooled fast reactors. I simulate sensors under extreme conditions in STAR-CCM+, improving simulation performance by roughly 2.5×, and analyze temperature data in Python to infer fluid velocity. I am also studying near-wall behavior of molten salt and its effect on accurate velocimetry.
