research

HEAT Lab Dec. 2024 - Present

Research assistant within the Nuclear Engineering department's HEAT Lab, led by Prof. Guanyu Su. My work focuses on the deployment of fiber optic temperature sensors for use in sodium-cooled fast reactors, which enable high-frequency monitoring and high spatial resolution. I conduct CFD simulations to understand fluid behavior and write algorithms to predict flow velocity from delayed temperature signals.

TCSLib Jan. 2026 - Present

Working with Prof. Venkatesan Guruswami and Shilun Li to develop a library of formalized theoretical computer science in Lean. I'm currently focusing on formalizing hypercontractive results for Boolean functions.

DRP Spring 2026

Directed reading with Sai Sanjeev Balakrishnan in Galois theory, following Milne's notes. I presented on Kummer theory and its applications.
